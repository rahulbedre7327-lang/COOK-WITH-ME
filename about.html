Who We Are

Cook With Me is a smart recipe recommendation platform that helps users cook delicious meals from ingredients available at home. Instead of buying extra groceries, users simply choose their ingredients and instantly receive matching recipes.

🎯 Our Mission

To reduce food waste and make cooking easier by providing quick, healthy and delicious recipes using available ingredients.
