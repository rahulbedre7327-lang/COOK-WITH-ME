Pani Puri

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9 Rating | ⏱ 40 Minutes | 🍽 5 Servings

Category

Snacks / Street Food

Difficulty

Medium

Calories

180 kcal

Cooking Style

Assembled Snack

📋 Ingredients

  • 20 Ready-made Puri
  • 3 Boiled Potatoes
  • ½ Cup Boiled Chickpeas
  • 1 Onion (Finely Chopped)
  • Fresh Coriander
  • Salt to Taste
  • Chaat Masala
  • Red Chilli Powder

🥣 Ingredients for Pani

  • 1 Cup Mint Leaves
  • 1 Cup Coriander Leaves
  • 2 Green Chillies
  • 1-inch Ginger
  • 1 Lemon
  • 1 tsp Roasted Cumin Powder
  • Black Salt
  • 4 Cups Chilled Water

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Mixer Grinder
  • Mixing Bowl
  • Serving Bowl
  • Spoon
  • Knife
  • Serving Plate

📖 Introduction

Pani Puri, also known as Golgappa or Puchka, is one of India's most famous street foods. Crispy puris are filled with spicy potato stuffing and tangy mint-flavored water, creating a delicious burst of flavors.


🔬 Principle

The crisp puri should remain crunchy until serving. The spicy potato filling and chilled mint water combine to create the perfect balance of sweet, sour, spicy and refreshing flavors.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Boil potatoes and mash them.
  2. Add chickpeas, salt and chaat masala.
  3. Mix well to prepare stuffing.
  4. Grind mint, coriander, ginger and green chillies.
  5. Add cumin powder, black salt and lemon juice.
  6. Mix with chilled water.
  7. Strain the pani if required.
  8. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  9. Make a small hole in each puri.
  10. Fill with potato mixture.
  11. Add chopped onions if desired.
  12. Fill with chilled pani.
  13. Serve immediately.

⚠ Precautions

  • Use fresh mint and coriander.
  • Do not overfill the puris.
  • Keep pani chilled.
  • Serve immediately after filling.
  • Use crispy puris only.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Add tamarind chutney for sweet flavor.
  • Use ice cubes for extra chilled pani.
  • Prepare pani a few hours before serving.
  • Sprinkle sev for extra crunch.
