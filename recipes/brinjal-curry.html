Brinjal Curry

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9 Rating | ⏱ 30 Minutes | 🍽 4 Servings

Category

Main Course

Difficulty

Easy

Calories

170 kcal

Cooking Style

Vegetarian Curry

📋 Ingredients

  • 300 g Fresh Brinjals (Eggplant)
  • 2 Onions (Sliced)
  • 2 Tomatoes (Chopped)
  • 2 Green Chillies
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander Powder
  • ½ tsp Garam Masala
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • Salt to Taste
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Cooking Pan
  • Knife
  • Cutting Board
  • Cooking Spoon
  • Mixing Bowl
  • Serving Bowl

📖 Introduction

Brinjal Curry is a traditional Indian dish prepared using fresh eggplants cooked with onions, tomatoes and aromatic spices. It is soft, flavorful and goes perfectly with chapati, roti or steamed rice.


🔬 Principle

Brinjals absorb spices easily while cooking. Slow cooking in onion-tomato gravy enhances their natural flavour and gives the curry a rich texture.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Wash the brinjals thoroughly.
  2. Cut into medium-sized pieces.
  3. Keep the pieces in salt water for 10 minutes to prevent discoloration.
  4. Heat oil in a cooking pan.
  5. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.
  6. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for 2 minutes.
  7. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
  8. Add turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder and salt.
  9. Mix well until oil separates.
  10. Add the brinjal pieces.
  11. Mix gently and cook for 10 minutes.
  12. Add little water and cover with a lid.
  13. Cook for another 10–12 minutes.
  14. Add garam masala and coriander leaves.
  15. Serve hot.

⚠ Precautions

  • Use fresh and tender brinjals.
  • Do not overcook the vegetables.
  • Cook on medium flame.
  • Stir gently to avoid breaking the brinjal pieces.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Add roasted peanuts for extra flavour.
  • Use sesame powder for authentic taste.
  • Fresh coriander improves aroma.
  • A little lemon juice enhances the flavour.
