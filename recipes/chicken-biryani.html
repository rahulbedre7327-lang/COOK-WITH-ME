Chicken Biryani

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9 Rating | ⏱ 1hr 20min Minutes | 🍽 3 Servings

Category

Main Course

Difficulty

Medium

Calories

520 kcal

Cooking Style

Dum Cooking

📋 Ingredients

  • Chicken: 1 kg, cut into pieces
  • Basmati rice: 2 cups
  • Yogurt: 2/3 cup
  • Onions: 2 large, thinly sliced
  • Ginger-garlic paste: 1¼ tbsp
  • Green chilies: 2, slit
  • Spices: 1–1½ tsp red chili powder, 1–1½ tsp biryani masala, ½ tsp cardamom powder, ¼ tsp turmeric
  • Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
  • Fresh herbs: Mint and coriander leaves, chopped
  • Ghee or oil: ¼ cup + 2 tbsp for layering
  • Saffron strands: soaked in 3 tbsp warm milk
  • Salt: to taste

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Pressure Cooker / Heavy Pot
  • Mixing Bowl
  • Knife
  • Cutting Board
  • Cooking Spoon
  • Measuring Cup

📖 Introduction

Like Dislike Hyderabadi chicken biryani is made by marinating chicken, partially cooking basmati rice, layering them with fried onions and herbs, and slow-cooking (dum) to infuse flavors.


🔬 Principle

Dum cooking allows steam to remain inside the pot, making the rice soft and the chicken juicy while blending all spices perfectly.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Step 1: Marinate the Chicken In a bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices, lemon juice, green chilies, and salt. Add chicken pieces and coat thoroughly. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight for best flavor and tenderness.
  2. Step 2: Prepare Fried Onions Thinly slice onions and fry in hot ghee or oil until golden brown and crisp, stirring frequently. Drain on paper towels and reserve the flavored oil.
  3. Step 3: Cook the Rice Rinse basmati rice until water runs clear and soak for 30 minutes. Boil water with salt, oil, and whole spices. Add soaked rice and cook until 70–75% done (soft outside, firm inside). Drain and set aside.
  4. Step 4: Assemble the Biryani In a heavy-bottomed pot, layer marinated chicken at the bottom. Drizzle 2 tbsp of reserved onion oil over the chicken. Add half the fried onions, half the mint, and half the coriander. Spread the partially cooked rice evenly on top. Drizzle saffron milk and 2 tbsp ghee, then top with remaining fried onions, mint, and coriander. Do not press the layers; keep them fluffy.
  5. Step 5: Dum Cooking (Steaming) Cover the pot with a kitchen towel or foil and seal with a tight-fitting lid. Cook on medium-high heat for 10 minutes to build steam. Reduce heat to low and cook for 15–20 minutes. Let it rest off the heat for 20 minutes before serving.

⚠ Precautions

  • Do not overcook the rice.
  • Use fresh chicken.
  • Cook on low flame.
  • Do not stir frequently.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Use aged basmati rice.
  • Add saffron milk for better aroma.
  • Rest for 10 minutes before serving.
