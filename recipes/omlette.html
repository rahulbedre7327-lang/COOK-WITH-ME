Vegetable Omelette

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.8 Rating | ⏱ 15 Minutes | 🍽 2 Servings

Category

Breakfast

Difficulty

Easy

Calories

220 kcal

Cooking Style

Pan Fried

📋 Ingredients

  • 4 Eggs
  • 1 Small Onion (Finely Chopped)
  • 1 Green Chilli (Chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Coriander Leaves
  • ¼ tsp Black Pepper Powder
  • ½ tsp Salt
  • 2 tbsp Milk (Optional)
  • 1 tbsp Butter or Oil

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Mixing Bowl
  • Whisk or Fork
  • Knife
  • Cutting Board
  • Non-stick Frying Pan
  • Spatula
  • Serving Plate

📖 Introduction

Vegetable Omelette is a quick, healthy, and protein-rich breakfast prepared by whisking eggs with vegetables and cooking them on a pan until soft and fluffy.


🔬 Principle

When eggs are heated, the proteins coagulate and become firm. Whisking the eggs incorporates air, making the omelette soft and fluffy.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Break the eggs into a mixing bowl.
  2. Add salt and black pepper.
  3. Add chopped onion, green chilli, and coriander.
  4. Add milk if using.
  5. Whisk everything until smooth.
  6. Heat butter or oil in a non-stick pan.
  7. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
  8. Cook on low to medium flame.
  9. When the bottom is cooked, gently fold the omelette.
  10. Cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  11. Turn once if needed.
  12. Serve hot.

⚠ Precautions

  • Use fresh eggs.
  • Cook on medium flame.
  • Do not overcook.
  • Use a non-stick pan for easy flipping.
  • Serve immediately after cooking.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Add grated cheese for a cheesy omelette.
  • Include tomatoes, capsicum, or mushrooms.
  • Use butter for better flavour.
  • Serve with toasted bread.
  • Garnish with fresh coriander.
