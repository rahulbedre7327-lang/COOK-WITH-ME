Drumstick Sambar

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9 Rating | ⏱ 30 Minutes | 🍽 2 Servings

Category

Lunch/Dinner

Difficulty

Medium

Calories

175 kcal

Cooking Style

Simmering

📋 Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Toor dal (Pigeon peas)
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp Ghee
  • 2 Drumsticks (cut into 2-inch batons)
  • 1 medium Tomato (chopped)
  • 6-8 Shallots (sambar onions)
  • 1.5 tbsp Tamarind extract
  • 2 tbsp Sambar powder
  • 1 tbsp Oil or Ghee
  • 1 tsp Mustard seeds
  • 1/4 tsp Fenugreek seeds (methi)
  • 2 Dry red chillies
  • 1 sprig Curry leaves
  • Pinch of Hing (asafoetida)
  • 2 tbsp Fresh coriander leaves (chopped)

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Pressure cooker
  • saucepot/kadai
  • small tadka pan
  • whisk/masher
  • knife

📖 Introduction

Drumstick Sambar (Murungakkai Sambar) is a staple South Indian lentil stew where tender drumstick pods are simmered in a tangy tamarind broth, combined with smooth mashed lentils, and finished with a hot aromatic spice tempering.


🔬 Principle

The core technique relies on extracting flavor from fibrous drumstick pods by simmering them directly in acidic tamarind water. Combining this infused broth with completely smooth mashed toor dal provides body, while a final tempering of mustard seeds and curry leaves locks in fat-soluble aromas.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Pressure Cook the Dal
  2. Rinse toor dal until water runs clear.
  3. Pressure cook with 1.5 cups water, turmeric, and 1/2 tsp ghee for 4–5 whistles until soft. Whisk into a smooth paste.
  4. In a deep pot, combine drumstick pieces, shallots, tomatoes, tamarind extract, sambar powder, salt, and 2 cups of water.
  5. Cover and cook over medium heat for 8–10 minutes until drumsticks turn tender.
  6. Pour the mashed toor dal into the pot.
  7. Stir gently so drumsticks do not split.
  8. Simmer on low heat for 5 minutes until the stew thickens slightly.
  9. Heat oil or ghee in a small pan. Pop mustard seeds, then add fenugreek seeds, dry red chillies, curry leaves, and hing.
  10. Sauté for 10 seconds, pour into the sambar, cover immediately, and garnish with fresh coriander.

⚠ Precautions

  • Digesting fibrous drumstick skin can be difficult
  • chew only the inner soft pulp and seeds, discarding the tough outer fiber.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Remove drumsticks from heat once they are tender to touch; overcooking causes them to burst open and lose their pulp into the gravy.
  • Make a light lengthwise slit at the tip of each drumstick baton so the tamarind and spice flavors penetrate inside the pod.
  • Briefly sautéing whole shallots in ghee before adding water enhances the overall sweetness of the sambar.

🍽 Serving Suggestions

  • Serve piping hot over steamed Basmati or Sona Masoori rice with a generous spoon of ghee and potato roast (Alu Fry).
  • Pairs exceptionally well as a dipping accompaniment for Idlis, Dosas, and Medu Vadas.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • Drumsticks are exceptionally rich in Vitamin C, calcium, iron, and dietary fiber, supporting immune and bone health.

✅ Result

A rich, fragrant, medium-thick lentil stew featuring soft, juicy drumsticks that absorb a balance of sour, spicy, and earthy flavors.
