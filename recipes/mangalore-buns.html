Mangalore Buns

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.8 Rating | ⏱ 20 Minutes | 🍽 4 Servings

Category

Lunch / Dinner

Difficulty

Easy

Calories

340 kcal

Cooking Style

Stir-Fried

📋 Ingredients

  • 2 medium overripe bananas (mashed smooth)
  • 2 cups All-purpose flour (Maida) or Whole wheat flour
  • 2-3 tbsp Sugar
  • 2-3 tbsp Yogurt (Curd)
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin seeds (Jeera)
  • 1/4 tsp Baking soda
  • 1 tbsp Ghee or butter
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • Oil for deep frying

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Mixing bowl
  • fork/masher
  • rolling pin and board
  • deep frying pan (Kadai)
  • slotted spoon

📖 Introduction

Mangalore Buns (Banana Buns) are a famous Coastal Karnataka breakfast and teatime snack—mildly sweet, fluffy, puffed fried bread made with overripe bananas, flour, curd, and a hint of cumin.


🔬 Principle

The dish relies on natural fermentation and moisture from overripe bananas combined with yogurt (curd) and baking soda. The starches in the banana tenderize the gluten, while an overnight rest allows slow fermentation to yield a soft, bread-like interior that puffs up high during deep frying.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Mash Fruit & Dissolve Sugar
  2. In a wide mixing bowl, mash the overripe bananas thoroughly using a fork until completely smooth.
  3. Add sugar, salt, cumin seeds, baking soda, and curd.
  4. Whisk until sugar dissolves.
  5. Add the flour gradually along with 1 tbsp ghee.
  6. Knead into a soft, smooth dough without adding extra water (the banana and curd moisture is sufficient). Smear oil on top.
  7. Cover the bowl tightly with a lid or damp cloth and let it rest at room temperature for at least 6 to 8 hours (or overnight) to allow natural tenderization.
  8. Divide the rested dough into lemon-sized balls.
  9. Roll each ball into a small, thick circle (about 1/4 inch thickness)—much thicker than a standard puri.
  10. Heat oil in a kadai on medium heat.
  11. Gently drop a rolled disc into hot oil.
  12. Press softly with a slotted spoon to help it puff up.
  13. Flip and fry both sides on medium heat until deep golden brown.
  14. Drain on paper towels.

⚠ Precautions

  • Deep-fried with a maida base and sugar
  • consume in moderation if monitoring blood sugar or managing a low-calorie diet.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Use bananas with heavily spotted or blackened peels—the sweeter and softer the banana, the fluffer and more fragrant your buns will be.
  • Never roll the dough thin like a normal puri a thick disc (around 5-6 mm) is mandatory to create the signature spongy bread texture inside.
  • Fry on strictly medium heat. High heat browns the sweet crust too quickly while leaving the thick dough raw inside.

🍽 Serving Suggestions

  • Serve hot or warm with coconut chutney, vegetable kurma, or sambar.
  • Enjoy plain as a teatime snack alongside a hot cup of filter coffee or chai.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • Bananas provide potassium, dietary fiber, and natural energy.
  • Cumin aids digestion.

✅ Result

A thick, golden-brown puri-like bread with a soft, spongy, cake-like texture inside, featuring a subtle banana sweetness balanced by earthy cumin notes.
