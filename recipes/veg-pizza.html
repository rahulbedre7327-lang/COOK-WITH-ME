Veg Pizza

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9 Rating | ⏱ 45 Minutes | 🍽 4 Servings

Category

Fast Food

Difficulty

Medium

Calories

420 kcal

Cooking Style

Baked

📋 Ingredients

  • 1 Pizza Base
  • ½ Cup Pizza Sauce
  • 1½ Cups Mozzarella Cheese
  • ½ Onion (Sliced)
  • ½ Capsicum (Sliced)
  • ½ Tomato (Sliced)
  • ¼ Cup Sweet Corn
  • ¼ Cup Black Olives
  • 1 tsp Oregano
  • 1 tsp Chilli Flakes
  • 1 tsp Olive Oil
  • Salt (Optional)

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Oven or OTG
  • Baking Tray
  • Pizza Cutter
  • Knife
  • Cutting Board
  • Mixing Bowl
  • Serving Plate

📖 Introduction

Veg Pizza is a delicious Italian-inspired dish topped with fresh vegetables, pizza sauce, and melted mozzarella cheese. It is one of the most loved fast foods across the world.


🔬 Principle

Pizza is prepared by spreading sauce over a pizza base, adding vegetables and cheese, then baking at high temperature until the cheese melts and the crust becomes crispy.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Place the pizza base on a baking tray.
  3. Spread pizza sauce evenly.
  4. Add grated mozzarella cheese.
  5. Arrange onion, capsicum, tomato, sweet corn, and olives.
  6. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes.
  7. Brush the edges with olive oil.
  8. Bake for 12–15 minutes.
  9. Remove once the cheese melts and the crust turns golden.
  10. Allow it to cool for 2 minutes.
  11. Cut into slices.
  12. Serve hot.

⚠ Precautions

  • Preheat the oven properly.
  • Do not overload with toppings.
  • Use fresh vegetables.
  • Avoid overbaking.
  • Handle the hot tray carefully.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Add paneer cubes for extra protein.
  • Use fresh mozzarella for better taste.
  • Add mushrooms and jalapeños.
  • Brush garlic butter on the crust.
  • Serve immediately after baking.
