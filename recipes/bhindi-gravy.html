Lady finger Gravy

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.3 Rating | ⏱ 20 Minutes | 🍽 5 Servings

Category

Main Course

Difficulty

Easy

Calories

190 kcal

Cooking Style

Vegetarian Curry

📋 Ingredients

  • 250 g Fresh Bhindi (Okra)
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1/4 tsp Chilly powder
  • Salt As required
  • 2 nos Onion sliced
  • 1 no Tomato sliced
  • Curry leaves A few
  • 1/4 tsp Garam masala powder
  • 1/4 cup Thick coconut milk

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Non-stick Pan
  • Cooking Spoon
  • Knife
  • Cutting Board
  • Mixing Bowl
  • Serving Bowl

📖 Introduction

Bhindi Gravy is a delicious North Indian curry prepared using fresh okra, onions, tomatoes and aromatic spices. It is a healthy vegetarian dish that tastes best with roti, chapati, naan or rice.


🔬 Principle

The bhindi is lightly fried before adding it to the gravy. This reduces stickiness and helps the vegetable absorb the rich tomato-onion masala while remaining tender.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Clean the lady`s finger well.
  2. Remove both their ends.
  3. Slit open horizontally, leaving its upper portion unslit.
  4. Mix together turmeric powder, coriander powder, chilly powder and salt.
  5. Rub the above mixture into the opened portions of the lady`s finger.
  6. Heat coconut oil in a pan.
  7. Fry the above lady`s fingers in it and keep them aside.
  8. Heat up the remaining oil in the pan.
  9. Add onions and saute them.
  10. Add tomatoes and saute well.
  11. Add the balance mixture of turmeric, coriander and chilly powders, if any left.
  12. Add garam masala powder and saute, until the raw smell of masala leaves.
  13. Add curry leaves.
  14. Add the fried lady`s fingers.
  15. Add coconut milk and allow it to boil for 5 mins.

⚠ Precautions

  • Dry the bhindi completely after washing.
  • Do not overcook the bhindi.
  • Cook on medium flame.
  • Mix gently to prevent breaking the pieces.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Add 1 teaspoon lemon juice for extra flavour.
  • Use fresh tomatoes for rich colour.
  • Avoid covering the pan while frying bhindi.
  • Serve immediately for the best taste.
