Veg Biryani

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9 Rating | ⏱ 60 Minutes | 🍽 4 Servings

Category

Lunch / Dinner

Difficulty

Medium

Calories

430 kcal

Cooking Style

Indian Rice Dish

📋 Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Basmati Rice
  • 1 Cup Mixed Vegetables (Carrot, Beans, Peas, Potato)
  • 1 Large Onion (Sliced)
  • 2 Tomatoes (Chopped)
  • 2 Green Chillies
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • ½ Cup Curd (Yogurt)
  • 2 tbsp Biryani Masala
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • Whole Spices (Bay Leaf, Cinnamon, Cloves, Cardamom)
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • Fresh Mint Leaves
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • Salt to Taste
  • 4 Cups Water

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Pressure Cooker or Large Pot
  • Knife
  • Cutting Board
  • Spatula
  • Mixing Bowl
  • Serving Dish

📖 Introduction

Veg Biryani is a flavorful Indian rice dish prepared with fragrant basmati rice, fresh vegetables, aromatic spices, and herbs. It is a popular meal for lunch, dinner, festivals, and family gatherings.


🔬 Principle

Rice is cooked together with vegetables and aromatic spices. The spices release their flavor into the rice while the vegetables absorb the masala, creating a rich and delicious biryani.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil and ghee in a cooker or large pot.
  3. Add whole spices and sauté for one minute.
  4. Add sliced onions and fry until golden brown.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chillies.
  6. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
  7. Add turmeric, chilli powder, and biryani masala.
  8. Add curd and mix well.
  9. Add mixed vegetables and cook for 5 minutes.
  10. Add mint and coriander leaves.
  11. Add soaked rice and mix gently.
  12. Pour water and add salt.
  13. Cook until the rice is soft and fluffy.
  14. Allow the biryani to rest for 10 minutes.
  15. Serve hot with raita or salan.

⚠ Precautions

  • Use good-quality basmati rice.
  • Do not overcook the rice.
  • Use fresh vegetables.
  • Mix gently to prevent rice from breaking.
  • Adjust spices according to taste.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Use homemade biryani masala for authentic flavor.
  • Fry onions until golden for better taste.
  • Add saffron milk for restaurant-style biryani.
  • Garnish with fried onions and cashews.
  • Serve with onion raita and pickle.
