Butter Chicken

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.8 Rating | ⏱ 55 Minutes | 🍽 4 Servings

Category

Main Course

Difficulty

Medium

Calories

490 kcal

Cooking Style

Curry

📋 Ingredients

  • 500 g Boneless Chicken
  • 2 Tomatoes
  • 1 Onion
  • ½ Cup Fresh Cream
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • Salt
  • Coriander Leaves

🍳 Required Utensils

  • Frying Pan
  • Mixing Bowl
  • Knife
  • Cutting Board
  • Cooking Spoon
  • Measuring Cup

📖 Introduction

Butter Chicken is a rich and creamy Indian curry made with marinated chicken cooked in a buttery tomato-based gravy. It is one of the most popular dishes served with naan, roti, or jeera rice.


🔬 Principle

The chicken is first marinated with yogurt and spices, then cooked and combined with a creamy tomato gravy. Butter and cream provide the rich flavour and smooth texture.


👨‍🍳 Procedure

  1. Wash and clean the chicken pieces.
  2. Marinate with yogurt, chilli powder, turmeric and salt for 30 minutes.
  3. Heat butter in a pan.
  4. Add onion and cook until golden brown.
  5. Add ginger garlic paste and tomatoes.
  6. Cook until tomatoes become soft.
  7. Add spices and mix well.
  8. Add marinated chicken and cook for 20–25 minutes.
  9. Add fresh cream and stir gently.
  10. Cook for another 5 minutes.
  11. Garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot.

⚠ Precautions

  • Do not overcook the chicken.
  • Cook on medium flame.
  • Use fresh cream.
  • Maintain proper gravy consistency.

💡 Chef Tips

  • Use butter instead of oil for authentic taste.
  • Add kasuri methi for restaurant flavour.
  • Use fresh tomatoes for better colour.
